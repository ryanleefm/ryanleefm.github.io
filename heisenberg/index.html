About the model
The four solid edges of each open ring are strictly horizontal flow lines. Membrane-fitted polylines are visual illustrations whose surface interpolation may depart from strict horizontality. FPC links visually connect separated neighboring membranes and are excluded from the mathematical curve. Cell hiding propagates to descendants along the generation tree, which represents a subset of the full adjacency graph.
